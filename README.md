# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Intelligence Dashboard | Power BI

**An interactive business intelligence dashboard to analyze sales performance, product revenue, customer activity, and monthly trends.**

## Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming e-commerce sales data into meaningful business insights using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key performance indicators (KPIs), category-level revenue, top-performing products, and monthly revenue trends.

Designed as a portfolio project, it demonstrates my practical understanding of data analysis, KPI reporting, interactive data visualization, and business performance monitoring.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance through key business KPIs.
- Identify product categories contributing the most revenue.
- Analyze the top 10 products by revenue.
- Track monthly revenue fluctuations and identify performance patterns.
- Enable interactive analysis using country and date filters.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Performance Overview
Displays total revenue, orders, customers, and average order value in KPI cards to provide a quick overview of business performance.

### 2. Revenue by Product Category
Compares revenue across Furniture, Technology, Clothing & Accessories, and Office Supplies to highlight category-level performance.

### 3. Top 10 Products by Revenue
Ranks products by revenue, helping identify the products that contribute most to overall sales.

### 4. Monthly Revenue Trend
Visualizes month-wise revenue movement to help explore sales fluctuations and changes in performance throughout the year.

### 5. Interactive Filters
- **Country slicer:** Explore sales performance by country.
- **Date slicer:** Analyze performance within a selected date range.

## Key Findings

Based on the current dashboard:

- **Furniture is the leading category**, generating approximately 281K in revenue.
- **Technology is the second-highest revenue category**, contributing approximately 153K.
- **Standing Desk Converter is the top product** by revenue, generating approximately 52K.
- Monthly revenue shows noticeable fluctuations, making trend analysis useful for identifying stronger and weaker sales periods.

These findings describe the current dataset and should not be interpreted as forecasts or proof of the causes behind sales changes.
